Guitar, Guitar, and More Guitar!

To play the guitar, you don't have to be a professional musician or know how to read music. Anyone starting to play, knowing just a few chords, can already play a simple composition for themselves or friends. Of course, if you want to play more or less serious compositions, you will have to work hard on studying musical theory and guitar playing techniques, but the fact remains: the guitar is the most accessible musical instrument.
